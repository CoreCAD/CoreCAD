# CoreCAD #
*Open, focused - built on bedrock*

CoreCAD is an open-source mechanical CAD platform built on the proven foundation of FreeCAD.
It exists to serve engineers and makers who want a clear, stable, and disciplined environment for mechanical design - without the distraction of experimental, artistic or domain-specific workbenches that fall outside core mechanical CAD.

*CoreCAD is currently in an early incubation phase. The project is aligning struture, workflow and scope.*

---

### Why CoreCAD exists ###
FreeCAD is a powerful and ambitious project that explores many domains of computer-aided design. Over time, this breadth has naturally grown to include a wide range of creative, exploratory and experimental tools alongside its mechanical foundations.

CoreCAD takes a different approach.

Rather than expanding outward, CoreCAD refines inward. It curates and maintains a focused set of mechanical CAD workbenches, priorizing:
* Stability over breadth
* Clarity over experimentation
* Long-term reliability over rapid feature expansion

By building directly on FreeCAD's mature codebase, CoreCAD stands on solid bedrock while offering a more opinionated and disciplined experience for mechanical design.

---

### What CoreCAD is ###
* An open-source mechanical CAD platform
* A curated and focused distribution built on FreeCAD
* A project that values stability, predictability and professional workflows
* A foundation for serious mechanical design and engineering work

---

### What CoreCAD is not ###
* A broad, multi-domain CAD tool
* A platform for exploratory or artistic modeling workflows
* A testbed for experimental design concepts
* A replacement for every FreeCAD use case

CoreCAD deliberately narrows its scope to strengthen mechanical fundamentals rather than attempting to serve every possible design domain.
